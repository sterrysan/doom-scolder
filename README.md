# 🛑 Doom Scolder - YouTube Shorts Blocker

> **Stop doomscrolling. Break the dopamine loop with resistance, humor, and tough love.**

Doom Scolder is a lightweight, 100% private Chrome extension designed to rescue your attention span from endless YouTube Shorts. It combines strict limits, audible scoldings, and an anti-cheat challenge (the **Shame Slalom**) so you can't just cheat your way back into the rabbit hole.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

* **🛑 Hard Shorts Interceptor:** Set a daily limit (e.g. 5 or 10 Shorts). Once reached, the feed is locked with a mindful blocker.
* **🔊 Audible Scolding:** Plays a sudden audio scold as you approach or hit your limit to snap you out of the infinite trance.
* **🎧 Custom Audio Drag & Drop:** Upload your own MP3, WAV, OGG, or M4A audio files (David Goggins, Gordon Ramsay, or your own voice).
* **🏎️ The Shame Slalom (Anti-Cheat):** Trying to bump your limit when addiction kicks in? You must steer a live laser wire through an obstacle corridor without touching any cards. Take too long, and a thunderstorm dissolves your line!
* **🧘 Mindfulness Reflection Test:** Need to reset your count? Wait through a 10-second penalty timer and accurately type a tricky pledge. One typo sends you back!
* **🔒 100% Private & Local:** Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero external servers. Everything runs entirely inside your browser.

---

## 🚀 Installation & Local Development

1. Clone or download this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sterrysan/doom-scolder.git
   ```
2. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `doom-scolder` folder.
